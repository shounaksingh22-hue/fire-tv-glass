Glass

Your TV, explained.

A clean home screen with no ads, made for this TV. Everything you need, in two minutes.

Use your phone as the remote

The basics

Home button

Always brings you back here, from any app.

Menu button ☰

On an app: its info page (force stop, clear cache). On an input: rename it.

Back

Closes any panel or menu you opened.

Home screen

Recent apps

The big row shows the four apps you opened most recently. It keeps itself up to date.

For you

Popular movies and shows on the services this TV has. Press down from the recent row.

Wallpaper

Changes every night at 12 AM, never repeating until it has shown them all. Where the photo was taken is shown on screen.

Along the top

Apps

Top left. Opens a dock down the left edge with every other app, the ones you use most first. Up and down to browse, OK to open.

Tools

Free memory (shows what's left), a 25-minute focus timer and breaks, and Lock TV. A locked TV unlocks when its time is up, or press the ten arrows it shows.

Inputs

Switch to HDMI devices or Live TV. Press ☰ on one to give it a name.

Customize

Top right. Opens a dock of settings hanging from the button, like Apps: wallpaper, screensaver, clock, weather, what the home screen shows, and a bedtime lock. Up and down to browse, left, right or OK to change the one that floats out.

Settings

Shortcuts straight to Network, Apps, Remotes and more. Everything else is under All settings.

Phone remote

  1. Join the same Wi-Fi as the TV.
  2. Tap Use your phone as the remote above.
  3. Type the 4-digit code the TV shows.
  4. On iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens like an app from then on.
On the remote

Swipe to move, tap to select, hold for options. Plus volume, play and pause, typing, your apps, Tools, and sleep or wake.

Private to this TV

Each phone pairs once with this TV's own code and can't control any other TV. Some shared building Wi-Fi networks keep devices apart, and then the phone remote can't connect.

Good to know

Screensaver

Starts after five minutes of no use: aerial views of the world, or, where it is set up, Paintings, which turns the whole screen into a framed painting. Pick one in Customize, and how often the painting changes. Any button, on the remote or your phone, wakes the TV.

Feels slow?

Tools, then Free up memory. The TV also tidies itself up whenever the screensaver starts.
