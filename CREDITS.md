image id, are in `assets/paintings/SOURCES.txt`:

- *Still Life*, Pieter Claesz, c. 1625
- *Mishima: Morning Mist (Mishima, asagiri), from the series "Fifty-three Stations of the Tokaido Road (Tokaido gojusan tsugi no uchi)," also known as the Hoeido Tokaido*, Utagawa Hiroshige, c. 1833/34
- *The Garden of Palazzo Contarini dal Zaffo*, Francesco Guardi, Late 1770s
- *Under the Wave off Kanagawa (Kanagawa oki nami ura), also known as The Great Wave, from the series "Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji (Fugaku sanjūrokkei)"*, Katsushika Hokusai, 1830/33
- *Landscape with Rock and Fortress*, Domenico Gargiuli (Micco Spadaro), c. 1645
- *Poppy Field (Giverny)*, Claude Monet, 1890–91
- *A Sunday on La Grande Jatte — 1884*, Georges Seurat, 1884–86, border added 1888–89
- *On the Nile*, Eugène Fromentin, 1871
- *Shower Below the Summit (Sanka hakuu), from the series "Thirty-Six Views of Mount Fuji (Fugaku sanjurokkei)"*, Katsushika Hokusai, c. 1830/33
- *Mount Washington*, Winslow Homer, 1869
- *Magnolias on Light Blue Velvet Cloth*, Martin Johnson Heade, 1885–95
- *Husking Bee, Island of Nantucket*, Eastman Johnson, 1876

- *View of Cotopaxi*, Frederic Edwin Church, 1857

## Wallpapers (`assets/wallpapers/`)
From [elementary OS wallpapers](https://github.com/elementary/wallpapers), renamed. Licences per
photo are listed in their [copyright file](https://github.com/elementary/wallpapers/blob/deb-packaging/debian/copyright):
CC0 (Unsplash before June 2017), the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), and, for *Aurora* and *Daybreak* (elementary's
"Odin"), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) by Ryan Gorley featuring
the work of Brendon Porter. Places and photographers are in `assets/wallpapers/captions.txt`.
Nightly upkeep can add Wikimedia Commons' Picture of the Day on your TV. Those photos are
fetched on your Mac, keep their own licences, and are not part of this repository.
